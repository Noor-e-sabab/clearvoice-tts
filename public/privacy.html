Clearvoice

Privacy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Clearvoice is a static browser app. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, or Clearvoice speech-generation server. Text and generated audio are processed in your browser and are not intentionally sent to a Clearvoice backend.

Downloads and network information

The app's code and speech engine are served by the website host. When speech is first generated, the browser downloads about 92 MB of Kokoro model weights plus related files from Hugging Face. Those providers, Cloudflare as the website host, and your network provider may receive standard connection information such as IP address, requested files, and request time under their own policies. The app does not control their logs or retention.

Browser storage and audio

Your browser may cache model files to avoid downloading them again. Browser caches can be cleared or evicted by the browser. Generated audio is held in the current page session and is not uploaded by the app; downloads you choose to save are stored according to your device and browser settings. Closing the page releases the app's temporary audio URL.

Your choices

Do not enter confidential, sensitive, or identifying information. You can clear site data and cached files in your browser settings. You can also choose not to use the app if the provider connections described above are not acceptable to you.

Contact

For questions, accessibility feedback, or to report a problem, email clearvoice.tts@gmail.com. Please avoid including private text or generated audio unless needed to explain an issue.

Changes and provider policies

This notice describes the current static release. The site may change as the project is updated. Review the privacy information from Cloudflare and Hugging Face for their handling of connection data.

This notice is informational and is not legal advice. If the app's architecture, analytics, or data handling changes, update this notice before release.
