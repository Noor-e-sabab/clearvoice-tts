Clearvoice

Acceptable use

Use generated speech responsibly. Do not use Clearvoice to impersonate a real person, commit fraud, harass someone, deceive listeners, or violate a law or platform rule.

Disclose synthetic audio when required by the platform, law, or context. Respect people's rights and get appropriate permission before using someone's identity or personal data.

This policy is a reminder, not technical enforcement. Speech is synthesized on your device; this static app does not provide accounts or server-side moderation.
