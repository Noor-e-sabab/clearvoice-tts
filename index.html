About Clearvoice

Speech, made simple.

Clearvoice turns written words into listenable audio with the open-weight Kokoro model and preset voices. Voice labels describe documented accent and vocal traits; they do not verify age or identity.

How to use it

Enter up to 1,500 characters, choose a preset and pace, confirm responsible use, then select Generate speech. Listen or download the result.

Privacy in brief

Speech generation runs on this device. Model and app files are downloaded from their providers; network providers receive ordinary connection metadata.

Responsible use

Do not impersonate someone or use generated audio to mislead, harass, or defraud. Follow applicable disclosure rules.

Help, privacy & use

Contact and feedback

For questions, accessibility feedback, or to report a problem, email clearvoice.tts@gmail.com. Please do not include private text or generated audio unless it is necessary to explain the issue.

How this app handles your text and audio

The app generates speech in the browser and does not send your text or audio to a Clearvoice server. The speech model is downloaded from Hugging Face and cached in your browser. Your browser makes model-file requests to Hugging Face; your network provider and hosting provider can see ordinary connection metadata. Browser storage may be cleared or evicted at any time.

Acceptable use

Do not use this app for impersonation, fraud, harassment, deceptive endorsements, or other harmful synthetic audio. Respect people's rights and the disclosure rules that apply to your platform and location. This reminder is not technical enforcement.

About the speech model and limitations

Clearvoice is an independent interface and is not affiliated with the Kokoro model authors. Speech is AI-generated and may mispronounce words. Preset voices are not voice cloning or verified representations of real people. The first model download is about 92 MB plus supporting files and requires internet access; the browser may cache them afterwards. Performance depends on the visitor's browser, device, memory, and processor.
